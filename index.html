About Us

The Story Behind PROLOGUE

PROLOGUE is the official reading club of LBSITW — a space founded by students who believe that every great idea, movement and friendship begins with a single page. What started as a small circle of book lovers has grown into a vibrant community of readers, writers and thinkers.

🎯 Our Vision

To cultivate a lifelong love for reading and meaningful conversation within the LBSITW community.

🌱 Our Purpose

To create a safe, welcoming space where students explore literature, share ideas, and grow as storytellers.
